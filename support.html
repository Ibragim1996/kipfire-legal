Before you write

Please include as much as you can so we can help faster:

  • Subject line: short summary (e.g. "Subscription not restoring", "Login with Apple")
  • Device: iPhone or Android model and OS version
  • Account: the email you use to sign in (if applicable)
  • What happened: what you tried, what you expected, and what you saw instead
  • Screenshots: optional but very helpful
